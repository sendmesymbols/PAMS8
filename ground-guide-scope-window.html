The rail

True size: 400 × 836 px, the full height of the map on a 1600 × 900 screen. It never scrolls. Click a letter line in the legend to switch that layer on or off.

The window

  • Docked, not tied to the mouse: it stays at the right edge of the map and never moves with the cursor; the cursor card and the H chip are separate and unchanged.
  • V shows / hides it at any time the guide is on (Ctrl+V stays paste). It remembers whether it was open.
  • Reads the ground round the symbol being placed; with nothing being placed, round the selected symbol.

The map, layer by layer

  • G Ground: hills and their heights, ridgelines, ITG candidates (violet diamonds).
  • R Routes: MSR white, all-weather blue, fair-weather violet, minor grey, tracks dashed; railways; road numbers on the MSR; bridges at close range.
  • O Obstacles: canals and rivers, power lines; NO-GO ground is red in T.
  • T Trafficability: the going under everything (off = plain ground, NO-GO in grey-blue).
  • B Built-up: built-up areas hatched, towns and villages sized by people, the biggest named.
  • C Cover: trees and orchards dotted, nullahs and dry beds.
  • Kept clear: the closer the echelon, the more is drawn; Bn and Bde show only main roads, towns and strong ITG candidates, and no more than a handful of names.

What is real here

  • Live guide, per spot and echelon: the verdict, the share in NO-GO, the front blocks, clear ground (the S search itself).
  • Terrain bundle: every layer on the map, and the nearest and share figures in the legend.
