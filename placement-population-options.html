Population Around the Cursor: Display Options

The bundle now carries how many people live around any point: WorldPop 2026 people on a 1 km grid (summed from 100 m), with males 15–49, children under 15 and people 65 and over, plus every named settlement with its size and population. This page shows the ways the Placement Advisor could present it, using real figures from the built bundle at four sample points. Pick a point and a mode in the bar below; every option redraws.

Sample point

Question1 · Is It Worth Showing?

Yes, as a short line, not a panel. People around a position change the plan even when the ground is GO. It belongs under B Built-Up Areas (civil considerations), so it reads as part of GROTBC rather than a new block.

UseWhat the planner learnsModes it matters most for
Fires and collateralHow many civilians a gun or AD site puts within its danger and counter-battery areasGun Position, Air Defence Site
OPSEC and observationWhether people will see the position being occupied (dense rural and towns report movement)All, most for Gun Position, Signals / EW Site, HLS
MovementCrowded roads, villages to pass through, refugee and IDP flow along the MSRWheeled, Tracked, Road-Bound Wheeled
Landing sitesCrowds, loose objects, and civilians drawn to a helicopterHelicopter Landing Site
CIMIC and supportVillages for water, labour, medical help; how many children and elderly to plan for in an evacuationDismounted Force, every echelon HQ
Caveats to state on the card and in the brief: WorldPop figures are 2026 estimates modelled from buildings, not a census count. People are counted where they live (night-time), not where they work. The grid is 1 km, so a figure is for an area, never for a single house. Each line says "WorldPop 2026 Estimate" and the brief lists source, location and dates.

Proposed2 · One Density Scale for Everything

Six classes by people per km² within 5 km of the cursor. The colours are the B family (the B letter is #9a8f86): darker taupe for few people, warm light for many, like lights at night. They never use the verdict colours (green, amber, orange, red), so population cannot be read as GO or NO-GO.

Options3 · On the Drawing Card (280 px)

Each card is the current card (header + Why) with the population block under the B caption. Today the card shows B only for Road-Bound, Rail-Bound and Gr Based; every style adds a B row for every mode. E is a header flag that appears only when it matters. All of them are built: pick one live with placement.population.card (Settings → Placement → People, or Ctrl+K "people card"); the value is on each option. F to K are new.

Proposed4 · In the Ground Brief (G Key): Civil Considerations

The full brief has room for everything: rings out to 10 km, the age split, every named place within 10 km, the nearest city, how this compares with the province, and the sources with dates. Copy as Text includes it.

Rule6 · When the Civil Flag (Option E) Shows


A proposal: thresholds per mode, advisory only. The flag never changes the verdict or blocks a click in Enforce
mode. All values would live in rules.yaml population so they can be tuned.









Decide7 · What to Approve


Recommended: B (Density Scale) as the card's B row for every mode, E (Civil Flag)
for Gun, AD, HLS and Signals, the Civil Considerations block in the Ground Brief (with C's rings inside it), and
L (People layer) on the map. Everything is built and switchable, so this is only a starting point: try them live
first. A and D are lighter and heavier alternatives to B; F (Rose) is the strongest new one when direction matters.
