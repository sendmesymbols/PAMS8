What the card does badly now

  • Too narrow, too small. 290 px at most and text at 10 to 11.5 px, under the 12 px floor every Ground Guide surface keeps (GroundGuideTokens FS.meta). Why and Fix wrap into a tall column of short lines.
  • The answer comes third. The layout scores sit above the verdict, so the eye reads four bars before it learns the plan has a fail.
  • Colour carries the verdict. A 10 px pill; no icon, and the ring on the map is a colour only.
  • One check out of thirteen. The other warns, how many passed and what is still not checked are not on the card.
  • Times as text. "19 vs 12 min" has to be read and subtracted; how late the move is never shows.
  • Keys as one run-on line at 10.5 px.

Three ways to lay it out

Same data, same words, same keys. Each at 1 : 1 on a dark field.

B in every state

The card never shows a verdict it has not checked: grey "Checking" until the Gr is in, and a known fail is never hidden behind it. One-line states shrink to their text.

The verdict marker on the map

Today each posn that fails or warns gets a 13 px ring in its colour (Rule lines: Fail / warn). The same icons as the card make the link between the two, and a short tag says what is wrong where it is wrong.

How it would be built

  • PlanBuildTab._cardHtml and cardCheckHtml write the new markup; the .pb-card block in Widgets.css is replaced. Sizes and verdict colours come from GroundGuideTokens (TONE, FS, SURFACE), so a Warn on this card is the same orange as SLOW-GO on the Ground Guide.
  • The race rows read the trigger table (fit.whatIf.triggers: own min, En min, verdict) instead of parsing the short text; any other check shows its short lines as they are.
  • _showCard flips on the card's measured width (it assumes 290 today) and mirrors B's columns when the card sits left of the cursor. 22 px off the cursor and never off the map's foot, as now.
  • The rings: _drawProducts draws the icon marker (and the tag with M2) in place of the coloured circle; Rule lines still say which posns get one. A late move gets its marker on its route.
  • A short screen-reader sentence on the card (role="status"): "Bn Def, two up, fit 74. Too late: Moves in time, 3 of 5 in time. 1 fail, 2 warn, 9 pass, 1 not checked."
  • No check, verdict, figure or key changes. Card off still works (planBuilder.card: false).
