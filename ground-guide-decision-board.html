    Reading the board. GROTBC is a terrain appreciation framework, not a layer list. G, R, O, B and C describe
    what is on the ground; T is derived from them for one mode, one movement type and one season. ITG / MITG is the
    commander's designation saved with the plan, never a property of the terrain bundle. A GO here is a point reading, not
    a route.
