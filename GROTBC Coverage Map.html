Each heading in GROTBC.md is traced to the dataset items that hold it in the terrain bundle and then to what the Ground Guide shows: the cursor card (tooltip), the Brief (G key) and the legend. The status shows where each item stops. Taken from the code and bundle on 5 Nov 2026 (rules 2026.11-grotbc-v2) and updated as C, then the G, R, B and W sections and R road network density (5 Nov), then O and the rest (6 Nov), were built. Checked again against the code and bundle on 6 Nov 2026 after the ITG characteristics, the ways across the railway, transport hubs and underground cover reached the card; every feature kind in features/index.json now has a row (the ones nothing reads are marked Data only). The C styles are compared in ground-guide-cover-options.html, the road density styles in ground-guide-road-density-options.html, and the G, R, O, B and W card styles (one line, a few rows and two drawn looks each, switch sections.gStyle … wStyle) in ground-guide-sections-options.html. The feature tiles were rebuilt on 6 Nov with the remaining GeoNames codes (levees, camps, industrial areas, parks, named vegetation, mud flats). Card switches are at their Settings.json defaults. The whole guide stays off until features.groundGuide is turned on.

Dataset items the card never reads

The pipeline builds these, but nothing in MS/Engines/GroundGuide/ loads them.

Raster layers (all 15 read since 5 Nov 2026)

  • terrain, landform, elev_hi / elev_lo: the G line (terrain type, elevation, slope, landform)
  • route: the R line (route type, load class, MSR) and the check for the mode
  • bld, hut: the B line (buildings, kacha huts)

The engine reads class, dist_<mode>, soil, rci_dry, rci_wet, wet (_goingTileLayers), cc, bank (_coverTileLayers) and terrain, landform, elev_*, route, bld, hut (_sectionTileLayers), only for the sections that are on.
