GROTBC Coverage Map GROTBC

Each heading in GROTBC.md is traced to the dataset items that hold it in the terrain bundle and then to what the Ground Guide shows: the cursor card (tooltip), the Brief (G key) and the legend. The status shows where each item stops. Taken from the code and bundle on 5 Nov 2026 (rules 2026.11-grotbc-v2) and updated as C, then the G, R, B and W sections and R road network density (5 Nov), then O and the rest (6 Nov), were built. Checked again against the code and bundle on 6 Nov 2026 after the ITG characteristics, the ways across the railway, transport hubs and underground cover reached the card; every feature kind in features/index.json now has a row. The last Data only rows (km stones, canal structures, mines, kilns and chimneys, deserted sites, rest houses and veterinary hospitals) and the other unread kinds (culverts, runways, boundary pillars, air targets, named marshes) were put on the card later the same day, so no feature kind is left unread. Then the former Partly rows were completed wherever the bundle holds the missing piece (land cover and area concealment, trees, lakes, wet ground, riverbeds, chokepoints, junctions, fuel, banks, discharge, snow, flood months, landform and depressions, building density in C); four stay Partly because the data is not there (live flooding, a flood calendar, the GHSL urban degree in the browser) or the rules do not use it yet (banks in the going). Later on 6 Nov the detail grid (sections.detail, on) put every former Switch item on the default card: a small muted grid under each one-line G, R, O, B and W section, including going for foot / wheeled / tracked, the soil's cone index and the age split. The C styles are compared in ground-guide-cover-options.html, the road density styles in ground-guide-road-density-options.html, and the G, R, O, B and W card styles (one line, a few rows and two drawn looks each, switch sections.gStyle … wStyle) in ground-guide-sections-options.html. The feature tiles were rebuilt on 6 Nov with the remaining GeoNames codes (levees, camps, industrial areas, parks, named vegetation, mud flats). Card switches are at their Settings.json defaults. The whole guide stays off until features.groundGuide is turned on.

Dataset items the card never reads

Raster layers (all 15 read since 5 Nov 2026)

  • terrain, landform, elev_hi / elev_lo: the G line (terrain type, elevation, slope, landform)
  • route: the R line (route type, load class, MSR) and the check for the mode

The engine reads class, dist_<mode>, soil, rci_dry, rci_wet, wet (_goingTileLayers), cc, bank (_coverTileLayers) and terrain, landform, elev_*, route, bld, hut (_sectionTileLayers), only for the sections that are on.
