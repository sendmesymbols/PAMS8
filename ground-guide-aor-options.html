What the AOR scale leaves out, and what makes it geo aware



  

    
Left out on purpose

    
    
      
  • GO colour. Only NO-GO and SLOW-GO are marked; open ground stays clear, as on a sand model.
    • 
      
  • Per-spot rows (cover, people, speed, road density): they belong to the Spot scale and the Ground Detail.
    • 
      
  • Range rings, frontage boxes, the H chip: spot tools.
    • 
      
  • The enemy's plan. The threat marker gives where he comes from; options and timings stay the planner's.
    • 
      
  • Any figure without its reason. Each line under the map is a deduction, and its basis is in D.
