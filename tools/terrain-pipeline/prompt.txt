Map overlay checkbox
Show on map is in the Placement panel in index.html, and in the Placement widget (Ctrl+K and the Settings menu) as Show terrain on map.

What it paints: green is deployable, yellow is deployable but near no-go, amber is caution, and red is no-go. Areas with no data stay clear.
Per unit type: a Paint for selector chooses dismounted, wheeled, tracked, vessel (water is green, land is red) or generic. Auto follows the symbol you’re drawing. There is also an opacity slider.
Where it works: both 2D and 3D, under your symbols, and it moves with you when you switch views. I checked it on the live map, not just in code.
Limit: zoomed out below the bundle’s minimum zoom (8 in the synthetic one) it draws nothing.
Needs: the advisor enabled and a bundle loaded.
Why, how far, what is near
Each verdict now has a headline and a detail line. Examples from the tests:

situation	headline	detail
in water (dismounted)	Permanent water — no-go for Dismounted	Nearest deployable ground: Bare / desert, 314 m to the E
beside water	Permanent water 120 m to the W	No-go for Dismounted
in a marsh (wheeled)	Wetland / marsh — no-go for Wheeled vehicles	No deployable ground within 1.1 km
open ground	Deployable: Bare / desert	Nearest no-go: Permanent water, 580 m to the W
The distance layer only stores how far the nearest no-go is, not what it is. So the engine searches the class tile around the cursor for the closest pixel of the opposite kind. That search reaches about 1 km at the finest zoom. Beyond it the message says “within 1.1 km” and doesn’t guess. The cursor badge shows both lines.

Bugs found while testing
A distance tile arriving after its class tile gave a brief false “ok” next to a marsh. It now says “loading…” until both are in.
Missing tiles were treated as errors and retried forever, because the dev server answers a missing file with 200 text/html, not a 404. Many production hosts do the same. Any non-image response now counts as “no coverage”.
The phrasing “120 m W of water” read backwards. It now says “to the W”.
