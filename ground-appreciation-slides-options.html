What is wrong today

  • Blank thumbnails. Each slide's preview is the empty white page, so the Briefing's slide list is a column of white boxes.
  • Walls of text. A paragraph goes in as one block at 1.43 % of the width: five dense lines in the top third, empty below, then "(cont)" slides.
  • Dark, cramped tables. The Briefing's default table: white on dark grey, 1.07 % type; "20.4 km; 15.7 km past the front of the AOR" wraps to four lines.
  • No frame. No slide number, no section, the para number floating at the right; the cover is a heading and one line.
  • Proposals as text. "(proposal)" is typed after the words instead of being marked.
  • Content faults. Confirm on Gr lists the bridge class twice; ITGs jumps to "(2)" when (1) is a ruled line; "Assessment of Tasks" is a slide holding "a. DP 1"; two slides are both "Deductions".

The slide list in the Briefing

Today: every preview is the blank page.

After: each preview is the slide itself, drawn from its own content when the deck is made.

The slides

What changes, in short

  • One frame on every slide: section and para number at the head, the title, Draft, the deck, a notes mark and the slide number at the foot. The Briefing's own banner (classification) stays as it is.
  • Points, not paragraphs. At most six points at 1.85 % of the width (about 27 pt on a 1080p projector); the whole paragraph goes into the speaker notes.
  • Light tables sized to their content: hairline rules, a ruled head, banded rows, figures right-aligned.
  • Pictures where they say more: the going as a bar, lines of def as a depth ladder, strength as paired bars, the trace large on the apch slides.
  • Proposals tagged in the draft copy, left out of the fair copy until accepted.
  • Content faults fixed: Confirm on Gr de-duplicated, numbering kept when a para is ruled, near-empty slides folded into the next, repeated titles told apart ("Deductions: Gr and Weather").

For you to decide

  1. Style: A Paper, B Ops room or C Khaki. Or one for print and one for the screen, chosen on the Export menu.
  2. Section slides before Gr and Weather, En Sit, Own Sit, Time and Space, Courses and Plan: yes or no.
  3. Points on the slide, the paragraph in the notes: points are cut from the drafted text by rule (a sentence or a clause a point, figures kept). What you wrote yourself is split by sentence only, never shortened. Agree?
  4. Font in PowerPoint: Arial (on every PC; A and C use it) or Segoe UI (B). The export keeps native text, so the font must be on the PC that opens it.
  5. The trace on each apch slide with that apch drawn bold and the others faint, as on the Lt Apch slide: yes or no.
