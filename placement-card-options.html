Placement Advisor: Card and Legend Style Brief

The Placement Advisor judges whether the ground under a symbol is usable while the planner draws it. It shows a Card (the small tooltip that follows the cursor) and a Legend (docked bottom-left on the map). The data underneath has grown a lot (bundle v2, GROTBC), but the Card and Legend still show only the v1 fields. This page shows both exactly as built today and the proposed GROTBC layout, with every field, colour and rule, so the team and other design agents can suggest a style.

Compare live directions: Open the interactive card and legend options to compare three GROTBC densities and rotate the sample map with the radar.

G GroundR Roads, Tracks & Railway NetworkO ObstaclesT TrafficabilityB Built-Up AreasC Cover, Camouflage & Concealment

Relabelled 2026-11-03 (GROTBC.md). The first build read R as Relief and T as Tactical Features. Now G holds relief (elevation, slope, landform), the terrain type (Plain, Hilly, Solid, Marshy, Wet, Desert, Mountainous, Built-Up, Snow / Ice), vegetation, soil and bearing, and water sources; R holds roads, tracks, rail, crossings by class and airfields; T is Trafficability (changed from Terrain on 2026-11-04): the going for the mode (on foot, wheeled, tracked, air, others) cross-country, on tracks, on roads by load class and on the MSR, dry and after rain, with the month's climate as the season; B holds settlements, kacha huts and public services; C splits four ways (cover from direct fire, cover from indirect fire, concealment from ground observation, concealment from aerial observation). Sample lines below use the terrain bundle as rebuilt (bundle v2 with terrain, soil, hut and climate layers, named detail and routes.json).

Proposed2 · GROTBC Card, Ground Brief and Legend

The data is prepared and presented by GROTBC. While drawing, the Card keeps its header and the "Why" scale and adds the three GROTBC sections that matter most for the mode. Pressing G opens the full Ground Brief with all six. The Legend gains a GROTBC layer switch and the G key.

Not yet approved: the six letter colours, the legend layer switch and the swatches in its keys. Everything else here follows decisions already recorded in the handover.

2a · Card by mode

  • Order: header · Why · three sections (always in G R O T B C order) · Inside (lines and areas only) · Nearest · Intermediate Gap Rule · hint to press G.
  • One idea per section: at most two lines of text plus one graphic (slope bands, concealment meters, origin chip).
  • A section whose data is missing is left out, or says so in words ("class not recorded", "no water source mapped within 5 km"). Never an empty row.
  • The card stays 280 px wide, so it grows taller (compare 1a). Is that acceptable? See the questions in section 5.

2b · Ground Brief (press G)

A panel for the cursor or the selected symbol: all six sections, a header line with mode · 1:50k sheet · tehsil · district, a sources line, and Copy as Text for orders. Esc or G closes it. It does not follow the pointer. Lines marked "Planned" are not built (routing between two points is a proposal in GROTBC-Roads.md). Pick a month to see the season and after-rain going change.

3g · Known limits of the data (say them in the card, never hide them)

  
    
    
  • Load class is recorded on about 31% of road segments; otherwise the card says "class not recorded", never "tankable".
    • 
    
  • Field channels and power lines are mapped unevenly (none near Lahore).
    • 
    
  • Slope comes from a surface model, so cities and tree lines read a little steep.
    • 
    
  • Tank MLCs are estimates (Al-Khalid ~48 t, T-80UD / T-80 ~46 t, Al-Zarrar ~40 t). Class 60+ = tankable for every main tank; 50 to 59 = Al-Zarrar only; below 50 = non-tankable; width under 3.5 m is never tankable; a tank on a transporter (HET) needs class 70.
    • 
    
  • Road condition is recorded on about 4% of road segments, seasonal closure on about 540 and landslide sites on 71: "no closure" means "none recorded". Say "Where Recorded".
    • 
    
  • MSR is a rule, not an official list: motorway, highway, main road, defence road, broad-gauge railway, and metalled roads of class 50 and over.
    • 
    
  • Soil is a 250 m model (SoilGrids 2.0), not a soil survey: say "Modelled". The bearing (Good / Fair / Poor / Very Poor, dry and after rain) is a starting proposal.
    • 
    
  • Terrain type rules and the four-way cover split are starting proposals for the owner to correct.
    • 
    
  • Trafficability is the per-mode going table (rules.yaml) applied to the mapped class, slope and modelled soil, with the month from climate normals. It is not a measured trafficability survey: no cone index, no live soil moisture, no cross-country speed yet.
    • 
    
  • Population near a place (wp, wc) is a WorldPop 2020 estimate, not a census count: say "WorldPop Estimate". Official population only where recorded.
    • 
    
  • Huts are mapped 1:50k symbols (kacha / mud dwellings), not a building survey.
    • 
    
  • Climate is WorldClim 1970 to 2000 normals on 5′ cells (about 9 km): an average month, not a forecast.
    • 
    
  • Not in the NCGDB: schools, government offices, banks and named markets (77 markets, no names). Schools, banks and government offices come from GeoNames (feature kinds 147 to 149, src: gn) and say so; the pipeline step is written but the current bundle build does not carry them yet.
    • 
    
  • Routes (routing from A to B) are not built: routes.json gives named roads with from, to, km, load range, closure and condition only. Any route line is "Planned".
    • 
    
  • Road and rail lines also cover the Indian-administered side, because the data extent is the whole relief-map sheet.
    •

      Paste this prompt with this file attached to ask a design agent for options.
      
  
      

      ROLE
You are a senior interface designer for a military tactical-planning map tool (MIL-STD-2525D symbols on an ArcGIS map, 2D and 3D).

CONTEXT
The attached HTML file shows the Placement Advisor's Card (the cursor-following tooltip) and Legend (docked bottom-left) in their CURRENT style, and a PROPOSED GROTBC layout
(G Ground: relief, slope, landform, terrain type, vegetation, soil and bearing, water sources · R Roads, Tracks & Railway Network: MSR, road / load class, tankable, crossings by class,
rail, airfields · O Obstacles: natural, man-made, temporary, seasonal · T Trafficability: going for the mode (GO / SLOW-GO / NO-GO) cross-country, on tracks, roads and the MSR,
dry and after rain, season from the month's climate · B Built-Up Areas: cities, towns, villages with population, kacha huts, public services · C Cover, Camouflage & Concealment: cover from direct fire, cover from
indirect fire, concealment from ground observation, concealment from aerial observation). Read the whole file: the CSS blocks are the source of truth for
"current"; section 3 lists every field you may show; section 4 lists the tokens, wording rules and accessibility rules that must hold. All sample data is illustrative.
Modelled or sparse values must say so in the UI ("Modelled", "Where Recorded", "WorldPop Estimate"). Routing is not built: show any route line as "Planned".

TASK
Propose THREE distinct style options for the Card, the Ground Brief panel and the Legend (with the GROTBC layer switch). Options must differ in more than colour:
different hierarchy, density and section treatment. Suggested spread: (1) calm and dense, (2) high-contrast and glanceable, (3) your own best idea.

HARD CONSTRAINTS (do not break)
- Card 280 px wide, Legend 290 px wide, dark card over a busy satellite map, readable in sunlight and at night.
- The Card follows the cursor and must never cover it, take the pointer or reflow on mouse-move. One idea per section: at most two lines of text plus one graphic.
- Verdict colours (ok, near, caution, partial, in) and the GO / SLOW-GO / NO-GO scale keep their meaning. The six GROTBC accents must stay clearly apart from them
  (flag any clash, for example the C green against the "ok" green).
- Wording: Title Case, military terms exactly as given (Suitable Position, Unsuitable Position, SLOW-GO, NO-GO, Marginal, Quick Fix, Intermediate Gap Rule,
  Adjacent to X / Encroaching on X, Partially Obstructed). Mode names as in section 3c. No symbols or dashes inside sentences.
- Built with DOM nodes and CSS only (no images, no web fonts, no framework). Works on touch and with a screen reader. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Never leave an empty row: say what is missing ("Class not recorded").

DELIVERABLES (for each option)
1. Name and a one-paragraph rationale (who it serves best, what it trades away).
2. A token table: colours (hex), type scale, spacing, radius, borders.
3. A self-contained HTML + CSS mock of: (a) the Card for Tracked Vehicle in caution and in partial with its three sections (G R O, from the mode table in section 3c),
   and the Card for Gun Position (G R C) to show the four-way C section;
   (b) the Ground Brief panel with all six sections; (c) the Legend with the layer switch showing the R layer and the C layer.
4. Accessibility notes: contrast ratios of the key text and badge colours, and how colour is not the only signal.
5. Implementation cost: which existing classes change, and what is new.

THEN
Compare the three options against the acceptance checklist in the file (280 px, no reflow, Title Case, works with missing data) and recommend one, with the single biggest risk.
