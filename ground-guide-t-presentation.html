Presenting Trafficability (T)

Is T shown in GroundGuide.ts? Yes, but in pieces. The card shows four separate things that are all T: the Cross-Country going band, the Movement Freedom rose, the Road band, and the Rain pill in the header. Each was built on its own, so they don't read as one answer. Below are eight ways to present T as a single, clear block. Pick one card style, where it sits, and the extras, then copy your choices at the end.

All built in the app (2026-11-05): ⚙ Settings → Trafficability (T) → T on the card (separate pieces, T1 to T8) and Where T sits (after Why, GROTBC order, folded). The extras are the existing switches: rain pill, verdict follows the season, legend season switch, overlay follows the season; the Ground Brief (G) already has the T sections.
