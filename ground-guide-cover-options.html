Cover & Concealment on the Card: Display Options

C in GROTBC is now on the Ground Guide card. Every style below is built. Switch between them live with groundGuide.cover.card (Settings → Ground Guide → Cover & Concealment (C), or Ctrl+K "cover"). Pick one, and answer the questions at the end. The Ground Brief (G key) always shows C in full, whatever you pick here.

What the card reads: the ground class under the symbol and rules.json grotbc.coverSplit (what each kind of ground gives against direct fire, indirect fire, ground observation and aerial observation, None / Poor / Fair / Good). That is raised by a river or canal bank within 75 m (bank layer and grotbc.banks). The local cover and concealment density (cc layer) is shown beside the class split, not mixed into it. The search looks out to 500 m (cover.radiusM) for the nearest Fair or better. Readings below come from the engine at five real places. The levels are a starting proposal, so every card says "Proposal".

3 · What Each Mode Needs (Proposal)

The field marked on the card, and the one the rose (C6) shades. From COVER_FOCUS in MS/Engines/GroundGuide/GroundGuideCover.ts. Correct it if it is wrong.

4 · The Levels Behind It (Proposal)

The four-way split per ground class, from tools/terrain-pipeline/rules.yaml coverSplit. Classes not listed give none. You set the bund row on 2026-11-04. The bank rows come from rules.yaml banks.

Decided 5 Nov 2026: the spot, not the area. The Kharian sample is a road running through trees. The class split gives the road nothing, but the cc layer says concealment Good. That is because cc is built from the vegetation-symbol density of the surrounding km² (classify.py cover_layers), so it describes the area, not the open strip of road.
• Never raise the levels to the area's value. Doing so would tell a planner a vehicle on an open road is hidden, which is the dangerous error.
• Never cap the levels by it either. cc does not know bunds or banks, so capping would undo your rules (on the Chenab bank it would cut Fair to Poor).
• When the area conceals better than the spot, the card says so and where: "Concealed round it, not on this spot: Good 31 m S (Tree cover)".
• On a road, track or railway it adds that a canopy over the road may screen it from the air. The data cannot tell, so check on the ground.
