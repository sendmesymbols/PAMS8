Road Network Density on the Card: Display Options

Road network density (GROTBC R, Road Connectivity) is now on the Ground Guide card. Every style below is built. Switch between them live with groundGuide.sections.roadDensityStyle and roadDensityLead (Settings → Ground Guide → Ground, Roads, Buildings & Weather, or Ctrl+K "road density"). Pick a style, the band rule and the circle, then answer the questions at the end. The Ground Brief (G key) always shows road density in full, whatever you pick here.

What it reads: the routing network the T road line and GeoCore use (network/gg). Every road inside a circle round the point is measured: km per km², all-weather and fair-weather (tracks, paths, roads flagged fair-weather), MSR and tankable. The ways out are where roads leave the circle. Crossings closer than 250 m along the circle count as one. Town streets, service roads and footpaths are not in these tiles, so a town counts only its through roads. The readings below come from the engine at six real places.

Place Circle Band R on the card

1 · On the Drawing Card (280 px)

Only the R section is drawn; the rest of the card is dimmed. "R on the card" follows sections.style (now per letter as sections.rStyle in the mapping session's work): one line is the default. Pick Thal or Astore Hills to see the band rule at work: there, tracks lift the total a band above the all-weather roads.

2 · Which Band the Card Leads With

Only matters where tracks and fair-weather roads make up the difference (75 of about 1,300 points on a 0.2° grid over Pakistan, at 5 km).

Why "after rain" is the proposed default. At Thal, 54 of the 93 km inside 5 km are tracks. Every road counted reads Dense ("room to disperse and switch axes"), but after rain only Moderate is left. Leading with Dense would promise routes that may not carry traffic in the wet. That is the error that costs the most, so the card leads with the lower band and still shows both figures.
