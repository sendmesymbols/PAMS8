# Operations of War Vol III, Withdrawal vs the withdrawal modules

28 Nov 2026. The book (GSP 6-100-0, 2006; `Material/Literature/1. Books/Misc/Operations of War (Volume-III) Withdrawal.pdf`,
78 PDF pages, printed page = PDF - 2) was re-read in full, checked against the modules that plan or write a withdrawal, and
the gaps were built. It was first read on 24 Nov 2026 (`Doctrine_Review.md` R78, R109, R202-R213). That pass built the
Bn, Bde and Div night withdrawals, the Bn withdrawal by day, the Bn delay and the Aprc's Withdrawal kind. Some rules it left
as confirm lines only.

Checks: `node tools/planbuilder-test/oow3.mjs` (72). Every Plan Builder suite passes, and `tsc` reports nothing in the files touched.

**Seen in the app:** a Div withdrawal placed near Pattoki, aimed west. The Ground row read Plains. Both int posns and the new posn stand behind water. Passage was laid at all three posns. Defiles on routes named each route's bridges. Before daylight gave 12 h 47 min of darkness tonight, with 2 h 38 min to spare. Both int posn view rows warn (9 % of the ground seen over flat Punjab with trees and villages); each view row also gives its front's height against the ground in front.

With *Delay means* on, the same plan in November read as follows. *Weather*: fog on 14 % of days, haze or dust 35 %, under 2 km 30 %, mean view 2.7 km. *Civil population*: about 1.4 million people in the 22 x 79 km zone, and Route C runs 370 m through a built-up area (warn). *HQ by the artery*: 80 m from a metalled road. *Delay means*: passes.

## Modules checked

- **Intelligent Deployment.**
  - Recipes: `bn-wdr-night`, `bn-wdr-day`, `bn-delay`, `bde-wdr-night`, `div-wdr-night`.
  - Code: `PlanWithdrawal.ts` (Bn night, the ground reads, Check my plan) and `PlanWithdrawalFmn.ts` (Annex P, the Bde / Div rows, the Int posns and New posn by switches).
  - Also `PlanDelay.ts` (delay, by day), the `withdrawal` figures in `PlanDoctrine.json`, and the tab's Check my plan.
- **Aprc, Withdrawal kind** (`AprcModel`, `AprcWrite`, `AprcBooks`). Its OOW3 cites (main decisions pp 6-7, factors pp 7-16, timings p 19, Annexes P / Q) match the book. Nothing to change was found; these are the Aprc session's files.
- **Page cites.** Every OOW3 cite in `MS/Engines` and `MS/Data` was checked against the book. All are right except one: "s9 pp 44-48" in two PlanDoctrine `searched` notes. It is now pp 43-47.
- **Not in scope.** The Ground Guide, Movement Guide and Move Planner have no tactical withdrawal. The Move Planner's DI Mov is de-induction.
