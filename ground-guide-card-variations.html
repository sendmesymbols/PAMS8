Card directions

GROTBC means Ground; Roads, Tracks & Railway Network; Obstacles; Trafficability; Built-Up Areas; and Cover, Camouflage & Concealment. Terrain type, slope, elevation, landform and soil sit under Ground; roads, rail, crossings and airfields under Roads; the going for each mode cross-country, on tracks, roads and the MSR, dry and after rain, under Trafficability. While drawing, the card shows the three sections for the mode: Tracked Vehicle shows G R O. Distances pair an arrow with a full direction name. Modelled or sparse values say so (modelled, where recorded, WorldPop estimate).
