Position defensibility score
ElevationSampler + viewshed + geometryEngine
For any point placed on the map, scores it across six factors: observation arc, fields of fire, cover from direct fire, cover from view, dead ground behind, egress options. Single composite score out of 100.
Decision supported: Is this the right position to dig in, or is the next ridge 200m back actually better. Objective score, not gut feel.
