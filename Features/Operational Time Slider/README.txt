How It Works in Operational Context
Phase 1 (Positioning):

Infantry units deploy to start lines
WEZ circles show initial fire coverage
Scouts in forward positions

Phase 2 (Assault):

Tanks arrive and take aggressive positions
WEZ expands with heavier firepower
Support units begin moving up

Phase 3 (Consolidation):

New units occupy captured positions
Defense perimeter established
WEZ now covers held objective

Phase 4 (Exploitation):

Reserve units committed
Pursuit continues
Expanded coverage to prevent enemy withdrawal
