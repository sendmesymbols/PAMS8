Extended View with IPB: the planned pages (v2)

Everything lives in the Extended View and is worked out only while F is on. Version 2 adds what the six books gave: forms of manoeuvre, types of attack, terrain-aware approaches, killing areas, two decision-point rules and sourced enemy templates. It also adds the controls that keep the view uncluttered.

⚠ Synthetic ground and illustrative figures: layout and pictures only. Doctrine figures are the books'; restricted handling applies.
New in v2
  • Declutter: the map starts Minimal (approaches, narrows, own Posns, threat). One-click presets, grouped layer switches, focus on one Apch, labels Auto / All / Off, an eye on every tile, and product switches: a product switched off is not worked out at all.
  • Apchs drawn as the MS attack arrows: MainAttack for the most likely, SupportingAttack for the others. Wide at the back, open back, inner line on the main attack.
  • Forms of Manoeuvre tile: open-flank test → penetration / envelopment / double / turning / vertical / infiltration; most likely and most dangerous.
  • Attack type and timing from the ground and the sun and moon; approach type by terrain; capacity and ATP criteria per Apch.
  • Killing areas, obstacle effects (block, fix, turn), counter-attack route, DP 3 for the counter-attack (launch while the depth localities hold), time phase lines, enemy heliborne LZs, season rules.
  • Own attack: Left or Right (v2.1): frontal discarded as dangerous; the flank that chokes the en MSR is preferred; Left vs Right weighed on IC s15.12's factors plus the MSR choke, with your weights.
  • The threat is the existing threat marker: placed on the map it is the en's start; docked it is the direction of attack; its arc is the corridor tolerance.

1 · Decide what is worked out and shown

Products are switches. Off means not worked out (no cost), and its tiles, map layers and rail line go. Kept per user.

When it is worked out

Every mouse moveThe spot, as today: fan, dead / screened Gr, lines of sight. ~6 ms measured.
Cursor rests 220 msBetter Posn; which Apch the spot sits on; how much of each Apch it covers. ~20 ms (est.).
Once per areaLeaving the area read, or a threat, echelon or month change: coding, corridors, Apchs, manoeuvre, times, NAI / TAI / DP. Worker, cached. ~150–300 ms (est.).
Only when shownFire corridors from every Posn, air, template check: only while their tab, rail line or layer is open.
F offEverything stops; caches and map graphics dropped. 0 ms.

2 · The tabs

Each tile has an eye (⊘ hide); hidden tiles become chips in the foot and the rest close up, so fewer pages to turn. Example: a Bde HQ being placed, threat marker dragged 12.7 km E, November, day.
