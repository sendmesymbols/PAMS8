Movement Freedom, Cross-Country and Soil on the Placement Card

The card today answers "can this unit stand here?". The new soil layers (texture, confidence, cone index dry and after rain) and the profiles' going let it also answer "how does this ground move?", both at the point and around it. Below are four options for you to approve or mix. Use the controls above (the ground, the mode and the season) and every option updates. All figures are illustrative, worked out with the starting rules in rules.yaml soilStrength.

Is movement freedom in the area worth showing? Yes, for units.
  • The verdict judges the spot. The commander's next questions are about the ground around it: can the unit get out, counter-attack, withdraw? Is it canalised by a canal or nullah?
  • It is cheap: 8 rays × 2 km on tiles that are already loaded (about 400 reads, synchronous, no I/O).
  • It changes with the season: clay that is GO when dry can close five of eight directions after rain. No other line on the card shows that.
Where it can mislead (say so on the card)
  • Soil is a 250 m model. The card always tags it Modelled or 1:50k Map.
  • The rose is an indication, not a route plan: it does not know bridges you will build or gaps already cleared.
  • Show it only for manoeuvre symbols (units, assembly areas, battle positions). Points, lines and areas keep today's card.

1 · Card options

All options are built into the app. Each one is a switch under ⚙ Settings → Card Sections (or Ctrl+K, type the letter's name). Switch on any mix, draw a unit on the map and compare; press G while drawing for the Ground Brief. The season comes from Cross-Country Going → Ground state (by month, dry, or after rain).

Each card is the real 280 px card with today's header, Why and Nearest bands. Only the new band changes. Under each card: what it costs in height, and its strengths and limits.

2 · Option D: the full picture in the Ground Brief (press G)

The cursor card stays short. The Ground Brief (G) has room for everything: the soil texture triangle (where this soil sits among the twelve classes), the strength gauge for the chosen mode, the three-mode matrix, and a larger rose with 1 km and 2 km rings and what blocks each direction.

3 · How to choose

My recommendation: A on every card (two lines, always useful) + B added for unit and manoeuvre symbols + D in the Ground Brief. Keep C's gauge for the Brief only: it explains why the soil fails, which the planner needs once, not on every mouse move.
