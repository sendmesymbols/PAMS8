Aprc Export

The appreciation of the situation, drafted from the map and written out as a formatted document and as slides. The tool writes what the ground and the marked plan can tell; the judgement stays the planner's. Every line is marked by where it came from, and every line can be overwritten.

Headings and numbering follow the Bn defence appreciation in Material/AprDf.pdf. The ground on this page is synthetic: the names and figures are invented to show the layout, not read from the terrain data.

On the map

1. What it can write

Change On the map above: the more that is marked, the more is drafted. With one symbol Gr and Weather (paras 5 to 11) is already drafted, its deductions laid out for your judgement; the rest waits for the enemy, the boundaries and a plan.

2. The Aprc sheet

The working window in the app. Left: the outline, a dot for each paragraph's state. Centre: the page as it will print; click a paragraph and type, and it becomes yours and is never overwritten. Right: what the selected paragraph rests on, and what to confirm on the ground.

3. The document

Word (.docx) and PDF, laid out as service writing: marking at head and foot, page number, address block, numbered paragraphs with their indents, tables, and the trace as an annex. Fair copy is what goes up; Draft copy adds the marks and the basis of every drafted line.

4. Slides from it

One command makes a deck in the Briefing module, a section for each part of the appreciation. Tables and charts are native, so they stay editable in the slide editor and in PowerPoint. Each slide remembers its paragraph: when the appreciation changes, untouched slides are refreshed and edited ones are flagged, not overwritten.
