Line	What it is
Yellow outline (a wedge)	The fire fan: the arc toward the threat, out to the weapon’s range. Red patches inside are dead ground, amber patches are ground screened by crops, buildings or trees.
Green solid line from the symbol	Line of sight to an ITG candidate that is probably in view.
Amber dashed line from the symbol	Line of sight to an ITG candidate that may be in view.
Red dotted line with a cross from the symbol	Line of sight to an ITG candidate that is probably not in view; the cross marks where the line is lost.
Thick red line with a dark edge	The main avenue of approach from the threat to the position. Orange diamonds on it are chokepoints narrower than the frontage.
Red dashed line with a dark edge	A second avenue, drawn only when it is really a different way in.
Violet line from the threat marker to the symbol	The threat’s own line of sight to the position: solid if it probably sees it, broken with a cross where it is lost.
Violet dashed outline	The 40 degree wedge the threat looks through toward the position; violet fill is ground it may see.
Green dashed line to a green ring	The better position nearby, which J takes.
In the rail only, there are also the GROTBC lines:

White circles: range rings.
Blue lines: canals and rivers.
Thin white or blue lines: MSR and all-weather roads.
Dotted tan lines: ridgelines.
White box or bar: the frontage.
