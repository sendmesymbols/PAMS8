How this was reviewed

  • In the running app at 627 px and 1024 px wide, drawing a Tracked Vehicle platoon round Jhelum: chip, card, every Detail tab, a NO-GO case on the river.
  • Separately, a full read of the Ground Guide UI code (card, Detail, views, overlay, indicators) with contrast worked out for each colour pair.
  • The two reviews were kept apart and agree on the items marked Fix first and High.

Keep: what already works

  • Three levels by key: chip (H), card, Detail (D), letter keys for tabs. The mouse never leaves the map.
  • Colour is never alone: every verdict has an icon and a word, every fact a dot and a word.
  • Honest wording: "Direction not known", "None within 2 km", "Map colours show X" and the Set by Hand line guard against a wrong read.
  • Pictures turn with the map, and the Detail never scrolls.

The biggest gaps

  • The word and the colour disagree: "NO-GO" appears on amber and orange (item 1).
  • The deciding word is the smallest text on the card, and the scale looks like buttons (items 2, 3).
  • The Detail doesn't open with its answer, and the same idea has four names and numbers across tabs (items 9, 10).
  • Red, green and orange also mean things that aren't verdicts: MSR, tabs, other units (item 16).
