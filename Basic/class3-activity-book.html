Bilal and the Little Kitten 🐱

One rainy day, Bilal was walking home from school in Lahore. Near the gate of his street, he heard a soft sound. "Meow! Meow!" A tiny white kitten was sitting under a tree, wet and shivering.

Bilal felt sad for the kitten. He took off his cap and gently covered the kitten to keep it warm. He carried it home and gave it warm milk. His mother smiled and said, "Bilal, you have a kind heart."

The kitten drank the milk and slept on a soft towel. Bilal named her Chandni, because her fur was white like the moon. From that day, Chandni became his best friend. Every day after school, she waited for Bilal at the door!

A. Answer the questions ✍️

  1. Where was the kitten sitting?
  2. What did Bilal give the kitten to drink?
  3. Why did Bilal name the kitten "Chandni"?
  4. What would YOU do if you found a wet little kitten?
