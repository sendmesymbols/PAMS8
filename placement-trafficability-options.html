Trafficability (T) on the Placement Card

Is T shown in PlacementAdvisor.ts today? Partly. The card's header and its Why going scale are already the trafficability of the point for the armed mode, but in the dry season only, and they are not labelled as T. Pick one option in each group below, then copy your choices at the end.

Now in the app (2026-11-05), all switchable: the going sections cover cards A–C and the Ground Brief covers D (⚙ Settings → Card Sections: going lines, mode matrix, season ribbon, movement rose, soil gauge, exit strip, going map, G brief). ⚙ Settings → Trafficability (T) adds the road line, the rain pill, a second scale under Why, the legend caption / Dry–After Rain switch / month stepper, the overlay and the verdict that follow the season, and the soil rule (cone index, soil bearing steps, words only). The rules in section 4 map to traffic.rain, traffic.header and the card section switches.

Sample mode Season

1 · What the card shows today

  • Shown: the verdict in the header (Suitable Position, SLOW-GO Terrain, ...) and the Why band: the ground under the cursor, the mode, the rule owner's sentence and the GO / SLOW-GO / NO-GO scale. That is cross-country trafficability for the armed mode.
  • Shown: the nearest NO-GO ground (Nearest), the Intermediate Gap Rule and Important Tactical Ground candidates.
  • Shown in the legend: the going ramp for the mode and its NO-GO / SLOW-GO lists.
  • Not shown: the going after rain (soil), the season, how the other modes would fare, and whether a road or the MSR carries the mode.
  • Not read by the engine: the soil, wet, route and terrain tiles, the climate grid, and the road lines in features/. Today it reads only class, dist_<mode> and the feature points (for ITG).

2 · Card: how to show T

The new band (or line) sits right after Why. The grey number under each card is how much taller it is than today's card, for the selected mode and season.

3 · Legend

The legend's ramp already paints the going for the mode. These options say so, and can add a season switch.
