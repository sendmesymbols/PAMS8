Platoon Guide: views that leave the map clear

Mockups only, nothing built. Same ground in every picture: a Pl being placed near a Rd, a likely mine / IED site at a culvert 340 m NE, an ambush site 600 m E, a canal 280 m S and a village 700 m SE. The dashed ring is the 500 m bubble and the lettered markers are what the map layer draws today.

The card sits on the ground you are placing onAbout 350 × 450 px, 18 px from the pointer. It flips to stay on screen, so it usually lands right over the Rd, mine site and ambush site it is warning about.
The reading follows the mouse to the tabsThe reading is for the point under the pointer. When you move down to click a tab, the pointer crosses the map, so the reading has already moved to the map's lower edge before you get there.
A turns pages before tabsA turns every page of Here and See before it reaches Danger. The open Pl Detail is 46% of the screen height, so you can't place in the lower half while it is open.

Keys (proposal)

A while placing or hovering: pin the reading at the pointer and open the Pl Detail on Here. A pin goes on the map, and the reading no longer follows the mouse.

A again: next tab, not next page. Shift+A: previous tab. A tab that runs long turns its pages with the wheel over the Detail, or a ‹ › at its foot.

Esc: close the Detail and unpin, so the reading follows the pointer again. Placing the symbol also unpins, and its Detail opens for the placed symbol as it does today.

No digit keys: 1 2 3 already open the Ground Guide's Extended View tabs, and 9 is the Mov Guide's water card.
