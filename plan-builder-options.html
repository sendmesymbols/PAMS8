Plan Builder: the Build tab of the Deployment Mgr

The Deployment Mgr gets a second tab. Samples is today's list of ready plans. Build makes a doctrinal baseline from the task, the unit's ORBAT and the ground. Press Place on Map and the plan follows the cursor like a sample does today. While the mouse moves it is a quick ghost. When the mouse stops it fits itself to the ground: positions slide out of NO-GO, dots turn green, amber or red, and Auto ranks the layouts. Click drops it. Nothing here is built; the ground is invented, but the checks are worked out live from it.

Terrain data Try: Build → 3 Place → Place on Map ↗, then move the mouse over the map and stop; drag the red Threat marker.

What it deploys

The unit picked from the ORBAT decides what is placed: its sub-units, weapons and their ranges. In this mock Coy Def and Bn Def work; the other cells show the order proposed for building them. Div is a frame only (Bde sectors, D line, gun and admin areas), not every Bn.

What to look at

  • Moving vs resting. While the mouse moves, the ghost is dashed and blue: placed by doctrinal spacing only, nothing checked. Stop for 0.35 s and it fits: a position in the marsh or the canal slides out (a thin line shows from where), the dots colour, and the card at the cursor ranks Two up / Three up / One up / All round on that spot.
  • Auto (step 1 Layout) changes the shape of the ghost itself when you stop: it draws whichever layout scores best on that ground.
  • The plan faces the threat. Drag the red Threat marker (top of the map) east: the plan turns, A3 becomes the main approach, and the ranking changes. Step 2 can set it to face north as drawn.
  • Echelon. Step 1 → Coy shows a Rifle Coy (three Pls, 60 mm Mor, GLHMG) at true size; Bn shows the Std Inf Bn (four Rifle Coys, BS ATGM, 81 mm Mor). + Pl posns draws the Pls inside each Coy.
  • Step 4 Check after the drop: the score card, every position with its reasons, J for the next best spot of the selected one, what-ifs (shelling, the en switching approach) and the trigger table.
  • Terrain data → Off: the ghost still follows the cursor and drops, laid by geometry only. Approaches, Auto, sliding and ground checks are hidden; dots are grey "not checked", never green.
  • Step 5 → Save as sample puts the built plan into the Samples tab, where it places like any other sample.
